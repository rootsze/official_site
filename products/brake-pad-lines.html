Automotive Brake Pad Turnkey Production Lines & Machinery

Direct factory-backed manufacturing lines, OE formulas, mold tooling, and on-site overseas commissioning from Zaoyang Friction Base.

Turnkey Engineering Hub
Located within the Zaoyang Automotive Friction Material Base (湖北枣阳摩擦材料产业集群), Rootze delivers end-to-end turnkey brake pad manufacturing plant solutions. From raw chemical batching to CNC dual-surface grinding, drilling, and packaging, we export machinery, mold tooling, OE formulas, and on-site technician training.
Manufacturing Workflow

5-Stage Turnkey Brake Pad Production Process

High-automation plant design with annual capacities ranging from 500,000 to 5,000,000 pieces.

01

Precision Mixing & Dosing

Computerized high-speed ploughshare mixers with chopper blades for homogeneous blending of organic, semi-metallic, and low-metallic friction formulas without fiber agglomeration.

02

Multi-Cavity Thermal Pressing

Hydraulic hot presses rated 200T-500T with multi-stage programmable breathing cycles, electric heating platens, and automated mold cavity feeding for passenger car and heavy truck pads.

03

Continuous Curing & Baking

Multi-zone temperature controlled continuous tunnel curing ovens (up to 250°C) ensuring complete resin polymerization, thermal stability, and elimination of gas bubbles.

04

Outer Arc Grinding & Multi-Hole Drilling

Specialized dual-spindle CNC outer radius profile grinding machines paired with automated drilling units for rivet holes, sensor slots, chamfers, and center expansion grooves.

05

Electrostatic Coating & Packaging

High-temperature electrostatic powder coating lines with infra-red curing, laser date/batch marking, automated shrink wrapping, and custom retail box packaging.
