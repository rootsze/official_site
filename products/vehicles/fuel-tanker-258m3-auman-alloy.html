25.8m³ Auman Aluminum Alloy Fuel Tanker Truck

Premium 25.8m³ aluminum alloy fuel tanker truck (Model HFV5321GYYBJ6A) built on an Auman 8x4 chassis. Total capacity is 25.8 m³ (effective volume 25.1 m³) with a gross vehicle weight of 32,000 kg and rated payload of 20,620 kg. Designed for multi-compartment haulage of diesel, kerosene, and gasoline, delivering superior fuel economy and corrosion resistance.
