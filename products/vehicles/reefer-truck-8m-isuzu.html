8m Isuzu Medium-Duty Refrigerated Truck

Medium-duty 8-meter refrigerated transport truck (Model HFV5180XLCQL6) built on an authentic Isuzu 4x2 commercial chassis. Features an 18,000 kg gross vehicle weight and 9,305 kg rated payload. The 8m cargo box utilizes closed-cell polyurethane foam with XPS composite bonding, food-grade FRP/CFRTP skins, and heavy-duty refrigeration units for intercity cold-chain logistics.
