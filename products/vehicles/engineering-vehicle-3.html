Key Product Features & Engineering Advantages
  • 1. U型箱结构，便于渣土滑落，密闭性好。
  • U-shaped box for easy spoil discharge and good sealing.
  • 2. 重心低，整车横向稳定性好。
  • Low center of gravity for good lateral stability.
  • 3. 油缸前拉式设计，消除油缸砸伤隐患，使用寿命长，可有效降低维修时间和维修费用。
  • Front-pull cylinder design removes the risk of cylinder impact damage; long service life and lower repair time and cost.
  • 4. 顶盖轻量化设计，小盖板随动结构，液压驱动，杜绝卸货卡滞和占用货箱容积，结构可靠、使用寿命高。
  • Lightweight top cover with a follower sub-panel, hydraulically driven; prevents discharge jams and does not take up cargo-box volume; reliable and long-lived.
  • 5. 侧板前后贯通整体成型，杜绝立柱内凸出积土问题。
  • One-piece full-length side panels eliminate soil build-up on protruding inner posts.
  • 6. 可选配智能物联网系统，喷雾系统有效抑制扬尘。
  • Optional smart IoT system; the spray system effectively suppresses dust.
