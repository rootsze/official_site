Smart Eco U-Box Tipper Superstructure (T700 High-Strength Steel / IoT Cover)

Next-generation intelligent eco-tipper superstructure engineered with T700 ultra-high-strength steel. Key Product Features & Engineering Advantages
  • High-rigidity U-shaped composite structure: Ensures clean cargo discharge and superior environmental sealing.
  • Low center-of-gravity rollover prevention: Enhances lateral stability of multi-axle dump chassis over unpaved job sites.
  • Front-pull multi-stage hydraulic cylinder: Optimized geometric force angles eliminate impact damage and extend seal life.
  • Lightweight hydraulic articulated cover: Linked folding panels prevent jamming during discharge and preserve internal box volume.
  • Full-length seamless one-piece side panels: Integrally molded without vertical interior ribs, completely eliminating mud entrapment.
  • Optional IoT fleet telematics & misting system: Real-time payload/lift monitoring combined with high-pressure dust suppression.
