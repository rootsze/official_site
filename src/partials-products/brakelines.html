Manufacturing Workflow

5-Stage Turnkey Brake Pad Production Process

High-automation plant design with annual capacities ranging from 500,000 to 5,000,000 pieces.

01

Precision Mixing & Dosing

Computerized 300L–1000L high-speed ploughshare mixers with independent high-speed chopper blades (1450–2900 rpm) for homogeneous blending (CV ≤ 2.5%) of organic, semi-metallic, low-metallic, and ceramic formulas without fiber clustering.

02

Multi-Cavity Thermal Pressing

Servo-hydraulic hot presses rated 200T–500T with multi-stage programmable breathing cycles (1–5 degas strokes), electric heating platen temperature accuracy ±3°C (140–180°C), and 4/8/12-cavity simultaneous molding.

03

Continuous Curing & Baking

Multi-zone continuous tunnel curing ovens (4–8 independent heating zones up to 250°C) with variable-speed stainless mesh conveyor, ensuring complete resin cross-linking polymerization, high shear strength, and zero residual gas bubbles.

04

Outer Arc Grinding & Multi-Hole Drilling

Specialized dual-spindle CNC outer radius profile grinding machines (tolerance ≤ ±0.03mm) combined with automated drilling units for rivet holes, sensor slots, chamfers, and center expansion grooves in a single clamp cycle.

05

Electrostatic Coating & Packaging

High-temperature electrostatic powder coating lines with infra-red curing (>240h salt spray resistance), laser date/batch marking, automated shrink wrapping, and custom retail box packaging with OE formulation support.
